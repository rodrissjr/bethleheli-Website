# Original itinerary photos

These files were downloaded from the public image assets rendered by Betheli's three source itinerary pages. The migration maps the trip hero/overview photos and the named accommodation galleries into Sanity image fields; no unrelated stock or generated images are used.

- Great Migration: [source page](https://betheliexpeditions.com/tanzanian-traffic-jam-the-great-migration/). Hero: `tanzanian-traffic-jam-hero.webp` (source `Serengeti-national-park-2.webp`); overview: `great-migration-overview.jpg` (source `wildebeest.jpg`). Accommodation sets: Lake Duluti Lodge, Tarangire Sopa Lodge, Olea Pamoja Farm Lodge, Ngorongoro Serena Lodge, and Karibu Mara River Camp.
- African Nights: [source page](https://betheliexpeditions.com/african-nights-circle-of-life-safari/). Hero: `african-nights-circle-of-life-hero.jpg` (same-named source image). Accommodation sets: Ngorongoro Lion's Paw, Intimate Camp Zebra, Ndutu Kati Kati Tented Camp, and Moyo Tented Camp.
- East African Safari & Beach Bliss: [source page](https://betheliexpeditions.com/east-african-safari-beach-bliss/). Hero: `east-african-safari-beach-bliss-hero.webp` (source `zanzibar_top.webp`). Accommodation sets: Hamerkop House by Lemala, Gibb's Farm, Entamanu Ngorongoro, Lemala Nanyukie Lodge, and Zanzibar White Sand Luxury Villas & Spa.

The Lodge/Camp images are the photo variants displayed in the source-page accommodation carousels. Their original source URLs were Elementor-generated image variants; the downloaded files retain the rendered image dimensions and visual crop.
